#  ShilpSetu

### AI-Powered Digital Marketplace & Business Assistant for Marginalized Artisans

> **From Tradition to Market, Powered by AI.**

ShilpSetu is an AI-driven, cross-platform mobile application designed to help marginalized artisans and traditional craft makers transition from traditional, offline selling to the modern digital economy.

The platform acts as a **virtual business manager for artisans**, simplifying product photography, catalog creation, multilingual content generation, pricing, inventory management, and market linkage through an intuitive mobile-first experience.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

**SIH Problem Statement ID: 26090**

**AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application for Marginalized Artisans**

Traditional artisans and micro-entrepreneurs often possess exceptional craftsmanship but face significant barriers when entering digital commerce.

Common challenges include:

* Limited digital literacy
* Language barriers
* Difficulty taking professional product photographs
* Difficulty creating product descriptions
* Lack of knowledge about SEO and online product presentation
* Difficulty determining competitive market prices
* Limited access to year-round digital markets
* Dependence on periodic exhibitions and physical fairs
* Difficulty connecting directly with larger B2B buyers and marketplaces

ShilpSetu addresses these challenges by combining **AI, mobile technology, multilingual communication, image processing, market intelligence, and digital commerce** into a single platform.
